## F-001 `017da62a81759656`

Lint levels are allow, expect, warn, force-warn, deny, forbid, configurable via -A/-W/-D/-F flags, attributes, or --cap-lints; forbid cannot be overridden downward and --force-warn takes precedence over everything.

## F-002 `8e715184141b7060`
