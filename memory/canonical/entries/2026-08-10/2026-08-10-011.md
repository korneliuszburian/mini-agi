## F-002 `a466ba1710d5520e`

RouteLLM data regime dominates router quality: trained only on the 65k-sample Arena dataset, the BERT classifier is worse than random on MT-Bench (CPT(50%)=78.09% vs random 49.03%); adding 120k LLM-judge labels (~$700) cuts BERT's CPT(50%) to 19.58% and matrix factorization's to 13.40%. High-capacity routers (BERT, Llama-3-8B) underperform in the low-data regime; matrix factorization and SW ranking are best on sparse preference data. (RouteLLM, arXiv 2406.18665v4, §4.1.1, §5.1, Tables 1-3)

## F-003 `59cba006261e2d6b`

RouteLLM data augmentation direction matters per benchmark: golden-label MMLU data (1.5k samples, <2% of training data) is what fixes MMLU; judge-labeled data fixes MT-Bench and GSM8K. (RouteLLM, arXiv 2406.18665v4, §5.1, Tables 2-3)

## F-004 `c8cefbf149a2c01e`

## F-005 `6b9a196c86ca045e`

## F-006 `bc112643c7fd6554`

RouterArena: all 12 evaluated routers fall short of the oracle; most cluster near '100% cost / 100% accuracy of their best model', i.e., over-rely on the strongest model and under-use cheaper alternatives. (RouterArena, arXiv 2510.00202v3, §6.2, Fig 7)

## F-007 `8a5af9a8ac9b67dc`

RouterArena efficiency leaders are classification/confidence-style open routers: vLLM-SR and CARROT save ~35% cost with <2% accuracy degradation; NIRT-BERT reaches only baseline accuracy at 378% of cost; MIRT-BERT reaches ~77% of its optimal accuracy at ~5× optimal cost. (RouterArena, arXiv 2510.00202v3, §6.2, Fig 7-8)

## F-008 `0e4e151b48280ed8`

RouterArena commercial routers (GPT-5, Not Diamond, Azure Model Router) reach higher accuracy but at significantly higher cost; GPT-5 ranks #7 and Not Diamond #12 overall — a restricted model pool (OpenAI-only) or expensive selections hurt the composite score. (RouterArena, arXiv 2510.00202v3, §6.2, §6.5, Table 2)

## F-009 `0eac49591541fe95`

RouterArena difficulty-aware cost allocation varies: GPT-5, Azure-Router, MIRT-BERT, Not Diamond spend much more budget on hard queries; others are near-flat across difficulty. Accuracy on hard queries (<=4/42 models correct) is often <10% for most routers — large headroom. (RouterArena, arXiv 2510.00202v3, §6.3)

## F-010 `3630725b5875a506`

## F-012 `cbb1986034a23ddd`

Established: routing between cheap and strong LLMs yields published cost savings of roughly 2–3.7× at ~90–95% of the strong model's quality for trained binary routers (RouteLLM), 20–40% fewer strong-model calls at <=1–4% quality loss for quality-gap routers (Hybrid LLM), and 50–98% cost cuts at equal accuracy for cascades that exploit cheap-model complementarity (FrugalGPT).
